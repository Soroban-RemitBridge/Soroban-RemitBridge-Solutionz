# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

This project builds infrastructure for people sending money to family, often
across borders, often to recipients who have no bank account. That context should
shape how we treat each other here. We pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Assuming good faith, and asking before concluding that a decision was careless.
- Giving feedback on the code and the reasoning rather than the person.
- Being explicit about uncertainty and about what you have not verified.
- Accepting that a design you disagree with may be a documented trade-off, and
  engaging with the recorded reasoning before proposing to reverse it.
- Writing for the next person to read the code, not the person who already knows
  what it does.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing technical discussion.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing a concern about money handling, privacy or compliance because
  addressing it is inconvenient.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## A domain-specific standard

Two expectations follow from what this software does, and they apply to code
review as much as to conversation:

**Do not treat a compliance or privacy requirement as negotiable engineering
friction.** No PII on-chain is a constraint of the medium, not a preference. If
you believe a requirement is wrong, say so and explain why — but do not implement
around it quietly.

**Do not overstate what has been verified.** "The tests pass" and "I ran it
against a live deployment" are different claims. Presenting the first as the
second is a form of dishonesty that this project has no room for, because the
people relying on it cannot check your work themselves.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, code
review, discussions — and when an individual is representing the project in
public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers
via GitHub's private vulnerability reporting channel on this repository, or by
contacting a maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, up to and including a
temporary or permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who do not follow or
enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent consequences as
determined by other members of the project's leadership.
